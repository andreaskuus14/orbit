# Third-party and owner-generated assets

## public/ui/storms/{purple,blue,red}.png
Cyclone textures for the Intelligence map. Cropped and hue-filtered from the three storm swirls in the owner's own
Higgsfield-generated reference frame (map-world.png). Generated by the owner, used here with the owner's permission.
Not third-party content.

## public/flags/*.svg
flag-icons 7.6.0, MIT licence. See public/flags/LICENSE.

## public/ui/frames/world.jpg
Text-free Earth plate generated by the owner in Higgsfield (map-world-clean.png), recompressed to JPEG. Owner-generated.

## public/ui/frames/{europe,estonia,tallinn,village}.jpg
Text-free map plates generated by the owner in Higgsfield (zoom-map-europe-clean, map-estonia-clean, map-tallinn-clean, map-village-clean), recompressed to JPEG. Owner-generated.

## public/ui/frames/*-depth.png
Relative depth maps of the five plates, computed offline with Depth Anything V2 Small (Apache-2.0, ONNX export from onnx-community/depth-anything-v2-small), 672x376 grayscale, white = near. Only the derived images are in the repo, not the weights.
